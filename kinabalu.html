Mount Kinabalu

An iconic mountain in Kinabalu Park.

Ranau

Malaysia's highest mountain

Mount Kinabalu stands at 4,095.2 metres above sea level and is located within Kinabalu Park. The Sabah Tourism Board notes the park's major conservation and UNESCO recognitions.

The summit experience is commonly arranged as a two-day, one-night climb. A local mountain guide is compulsory, and climbers should prepare for cold, windy conditions at higher elevations.

Before a climb

Plan ahead

Climbing places are limited, so advance booking is advisable.

Prepare

Bring suitable clothing, water, food and equipment for changing conditions.

Follow guides

Follow registration, guide and trail requirements from the relevant authorities.
