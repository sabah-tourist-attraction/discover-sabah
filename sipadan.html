Sipadan Island

A renowned marine destination in Sabah.

Marine

Underwater Sabah

Sipadan Island is one of Sabah's signature destinations and is associated with world-class diving experiences and rich marine biodiversity.

Because marine tourism is regulated, visitors should follow current permit, operator and conservation requirements.

Responsible diving

Respect marine life and avoid touching coral or wildlife.
