Semporna

Gateway to Sabah's famous marine destinations.

East Coast

Island and diving adventures

Semporna is on Sabah's south-eastern coast and is known as a gateway to diving and island destinations.

Attractions around the district include Bohey Dulang, Kapalai, Sipadan, Tun Sakaran Marine Park, Pom Pom, Mataking and Mabul.

Getting there

The Sabah Tourism Board states that visitors commonly fly to Tawau Airport and then travel onward to Semporna; bus travel from Kota Kinabalu is another option.

For island trips, book with licensed operators and confirm permits, boat transfers and current conditions in advance.
