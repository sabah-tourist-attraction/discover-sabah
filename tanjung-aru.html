Tanjung Aru Beach

A popular Kota Kinabalu beach and sunset spot.

Kota Kinabalu

Sunset by the sea

Tanjung Aru Beach takes its name from the casuarina trees along the beach. The Sabah Tourism Board highlights its sunset views and activities such as picnics and kite flying.

Nearby attractions include Perdana Park, Sabah State Library, Kinabalu Golf Club and other Kota Kinabalu sites.
