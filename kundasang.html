Kundasang

Cool highlands, mountain scenery and rural experiences.

Highlands

A scenic mountain gateway

Kundasang is a highland area in the Ranau region, widely associated with views toward Mount Kinabalu, farms and cool-weather experiences.

Visitors can combine a Kundasang trip with nearby nature attractions and local food experiences. Always check individual attraction opening hours and access conditions before travelling.
