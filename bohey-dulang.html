Bohey Dulang

A dramatic island viewpoint in Tun Sakaran Marine Park.

Semporna

Island views from above

Bohey Dulang is one of the well-known attractions around Semporna and forms part of Tun Sakaran Marine Park.

The destination is particularly associated with hiking to an elevated viewpoint overlooking surrounding turquoise waters and islands.

Trail access, weather and conservation requirements can change. Check current local guidance before travelling.
