About Sabah

A quick introduction to the Land Below the Wind.

Sabah

Best of Borneo

Sabah is one of Malaysia's thirteen states and is located on the island of Borneo. It shares the island with Sarawak, Brunei and Indonesian Kalimantan.

The Sabah Tourism Board highlights the state's nature diversity, cultures, beaches, adventure opportunities and cuisine.

Quick facts:
Capital: Kota Kinabalu
Currency: Malaysian Ringgit (MYR)
Timezone: GMT +8
Climate: Equatorial / tropical

Nature

Mountains, rainforests, wildlife and marine ecosystems are major attractions.

Culture

Sabah is home to diverse communities, languages, traditions and food.

Adventure

Visitors can explore hiking, diving, island activities and more.
